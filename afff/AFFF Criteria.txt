AFFF Criteria






Claimant must have been a civilian, military firefighter or Navy Sailor and exposed to Firefighting Foam (AFFF) after 1966


Claimant must have been diagnosed with qualifying cancer at least 6 months after AFFF exposure date


Claimant must have been diagnosed with qualifying cancer within the last 20 years


Claimant was diagnosed with one of the following cancers: 


Kidney Cancer


Testicular Cancer 


Liver Cancer 


Ulcerative Colitis
